## Mandatory guardrails

1. **Primary client KPI:** Meta-attributed purchase CPA must be **below $150**. Use the [JumpSpeak benchmarks](docs/CLIENT_CONFIG.md#ad-performance-benchmarks) to diagnose what to test. Treat 0.5× ROAS as optional context and rank creatives by purchase CPA.
2. **Performance source:** connected Meta plugin; **do not use Windsor.ai** for JumpSpeak account analysis unless the user reverses that instruction.
3. **Research:** firsthand recent evidence, dated and linked; distinguish observed comments from inferred search queries and organic claims. Keep the seven JumpSpeak buyer situations and awareness stages in scope.
4. **Creative inputs:** latest Fathom creative call, Meta performance insights and Foreplay direct/adjacent inspirations. Always link canonical Foreplay ads and show actual thumbnails where available.
5. **Testing:** no hard-coded 5-iteration or 20-concept cap. Current plan snapshot is 24: 9 iterations + 15 net-new, each with three distinct hooks, a persona, an awareness stage, dated scan evidence and reviewed Foreplay references. Review current queue to prevent duplicates. Preserve/repair winner logic anchored in CPA and diagnosis.
6. **Formats:** videos, statics, carousels, GIFs. 45–65 seconds is an appropriate video narrative target when useful, not a hard platform restriction; use clear product demonstration and action CTAs.
7. **Handoffs:** no briefing, production task, campaign launch or account mutation without an explicit user request. The user explicitly paused briefing in the latest project conversation.
8. **Production (future):** use canonical Growthub Sales Psychology and Production Brief skills when requested; verify Asana project fields, owners, assets and approved claims; do not copy Twillory's Monday schema.
9. **State integrity:** keep proposed, approved, briefed, assigned, footage received, produced, approved asset and launched statuses distinct. Never claim a completed handoff based on a proposed plan.
10. **GitHub governance:** maintain mirrored documentation in `BrandonGrowthub/JumpSpeak` and `Growthub-OS/JumpSpeak` when available, with public visibility as approved. Do not put confidential source files or tokens in these repos.

If a connector lacks an operation, state the precise limitation and prepare a verifiable alternative rather than claiming success. Verify repository contents after writes. Update `PROJECT_STATUS.md` after genuine progress.
