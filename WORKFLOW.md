# JumpSpeak Creative Workflow

This runbook adapts Twillory's seven-stage architecture to JumpSpeak's established 2026-11-23 strategy process. Read [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md), [PROCESS.md](PROCESS.md), [PROJECT_STATUS.md](PROJECT_STATUS.md), and the actual current plan before execution. **Documented policy is not evidence that a stage has happened.**

| Stage | Output | Gate |
|---|---|---|
| 0. Client foundation | Brand/product claims, KPI authority, current offer and production queue | Source verified |
| 1. Conversation Scan | Fresh, dated consumer evidence and opportunities | Evidence IDs, links and coverage gaps |
| 2. Deep ICP intelligence | Persona motivations, awareness stages, problem situations | Supported versus provisional profiles distinguished |
| 3. Ad Account Performance Insights | CPA-ranked ads and soft-metric diagnoses | Exact Meta ad names/IDs, thumb, spend, purchases, CPA |
| 4. Creative Call Intelligence | Stakeholder-identified formats, iteration directions, constraints | Call citation and verification status |
| 5. Initial Testing Plan | Iterations, near-winners and net-new hypotheses | Prior queue deduplication and source traceability |
| 6. Foreplay Concepting | Direct/adjacent/organic-style visual references | Canonical links, real thumbnails, no unverified performance claims |
| 7. Revised Testing Plan | Prioritized concept cards; 3 hook treatments each | Review, version and approval status |
| 8. Sales Psychology + Production Brief | Exact scripts/design/scene instructions and approved assets | Explicit user go-ahead; not yet started |
| 9. Asana Handoff & Production QA | Verified owner, brief, stage, assets and three delivered variants | Read-back from live Asana, no invented fields |
| 10. Launch and Learning | Meta ad/variant identity, attribution, CPA, lessons | Evidence of launch and sufficient test delivery |

## Research to strategy

1. **Conversation Scan:** Run Growthub canonical scan over the previous 7 days, extending to 10 with reasons; log individual post/comment dates, Reddit, platform reviews, social video/comments, competitor reviews, and access gaps. The existing 2026-11-23 scan may be used as historical input, not passed off as a new scan.
2. **Persona research:** Cross-map findings to seven JumpSpeak buying situations and behavioral overlays. Label buyer research as hypothesis where it lacks first-party conversion validation.
3. **Meta performance:** Use connected **Meta plugin**, not Windsor.ai. Primary KPI is **Meta-attributed purchase CPA < $150**. Review 14 completed days for established ads and 5–7 for testing where possible. Compare hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CPC and acquisition/scaling frequency with the [JumpSpeak benchmarks](docs/CLIENT_CONFIG.md#ad-performance-benchmarks). Bring the strongest diagnostic signal and one proposed change into the weekly testing plan. ROAS is optional context.
4. **Creative call:** Read actual Fathom recording/transcript. Differentiate stakeholder statements about performance from confirmed Meta numbers. Capture quiet AI narration, founder natural movement, correction demos, app UI, organic-style learning stories, and creator lead times where relevant.
5. **Initial plan:** Audit existing queue, briefs and live ads before commissioning. Make a provisional priority slate. Balance proven under-target ads, high-volume near-winners with actionable diagnoses, and truly distinct net-new jobs-to-be-done. No fixed winner-iteration quota.
6. **Foreplay pass:** Review direct competitors (e.g., Speak, Praktika, ELSA, Babbel, Preply, Lingoda, Duolingo), adjacent education platforms, and verified organic examples. Use any fitting format: video, static, carousel, GIF. Record actual reference media URL, thumbnail, hook, structural mechanism, and confidence of any performance statement.
7. **Final plan:** Publish an HTML plan with **24 concepts proposed as of 2026-11-23: 9 iteration, 15 net new**; this is a planning snapshot, not a recurring fixed quota. Each concept gets three distinct hooks and links to its persona, awareness stage, dated Conversation Scan evidence and reviewed Foreplay references. Show the actual reference thumbnails and verify source links where available. Record missing media or inaccessible links on the card. Rank by evidence, value of learning, performance and production capacity. Foreplay findings can replace or modify initial cards.

## Briefing and onward (NOT AUTHORIZED OR EXECUTED YET)

Only after explicit user request, load the canonical Growthub Sales Psychology and Production Brief skills and full references. Create separate creator-sourcing, editing, and design directions. Verify the real Asana schema and owners before assignment. Distinguish selected → briefed → assigned → footage received → produced → approved → launched. On launch, preserve concept/variant ↔ Meta ad identity, campaign, geography, date and attribution window. Feed mature CPA results and diagnostic evidence into the next cycle.

## Change control

Use versioned concept IDs and source links. When a plan changes, update cards, references and any future brief dependencies. Keep both GitHub repository copies consistent when authorized. No one may infer historical completions or create production work from this file alone.
