# JumpSpeak versus Twillory

**Reviewed November 23, 2026.** The JumpSpeak package records prior work. The source reports, concept cards and live Asana and Meta records were outside this verification. The comparison uses Twillory’s [README](https://github.com/Growthub-OS/Twillory/blob/main/README.md), [workflow](https://github.com/Growthub-OS/Twillory/blob/main/WORKFLOW.md), [process](https://github.com/Growthub-OS/Twillory/blob/main/PROCESS.md), [agent instructions](https://github.com/Growthub-OS/Twillory/blob/main/AGENTS.md), [status log](https://github.com/Growthub-OS/Twillory/blob/main/PROJECT_STATUS.md) and [Ad Account Performance Insights skill](https://github.com/Growthub-OS/Twillory/blob/main/.agents/skills/twillory-ad-account-performance-insights/SKILL.md).

| Workflow | Twillory reference | JumpSpeak work recorded | Requirement or implementation gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Conversation Scan | Rolling seven-day scan, documented extension to ten days, source-family coverage and dated firsthand evidence | Weekly scan and ICP-integrated scan created | Log coverage and access gaps each run. Keep evidence IDs and verified permalinks on plan cards. Establish the refresh cadence. |
| Deep ICP personas | Men’s personas need finalization and women’s segments are provisional in the repo’s status log | Seven research-led buyer situations and behavioral overlays | Validate against first-party purchase or onboarding evidence. Keep persona and awareness stage visible per concept. |
| Ad Account Performance Insights | Lane-specific ROAS targets, ad-level creative evidence, owner-only dashboard and spend-based test eligibility | CPA-first Meta review created for selected ads | Use the connected Meta plugin and **Meta-attributed purchase CPA below $150**. Apply the [JumpSpeak diagnostic benchmarks](CLIENT_CONFIG.md#ad-performance-benchmarks). Confirm full coverage, attribution, comparable cohorts and exact creative/media IDs, including original Ad 213. |
| Creative Call Intelligence | Stakeholder feedback enters the plan and handoffs | November 23 Fathom call integrated as its own workflow stage | Keep dated decisions and timestamps and reconcile performance claims against Meta. |
| Foreplay competitive research | Actual media review, visible thumbnails and verified share links on HTML cards | Direct and adjacent research recorded. The package reports externally hosted thumbnails on the plan. | Verify canonical reference links, thumbnail rendering and permitted fallback media. Record independent evidence before making organic performance claims. |
| Creative Testing Plans | Stable concept IDs, three hooks, explicit hold/change, queue reconciliation and spend-weighted SKU allocation | Proposed **24 concepts, 9 iterations and 15 net-new**, each specified with three hooks | Audit the actual cards and reconcile the live queue. Each card needs its persona, awareness stage, scan evidence and actual Foreplay references, with visible thumbnails and verified source links where available. Prioritize by CPA evidence, learning value and capacity. |
| Production briefing | Canonical Sales Psychology and Production Brief, role-specific directions, hook-level variants and asset checks | Requirements documented. Briefing remains paused. | When authorized, use both canonical skills. Confirm app UI, claims, destination, offer, assets and rights, and carry all three hooks through approved deliverables. |
| Production handoff | Verified Monday schema, named owners, separate stages and write read-back | Asana project identified and handoff requirements documented | Confirm actual Asana fields, owners, permissions and dates. Reconcile existing work before handoff. Validate the saved fields and links when production work is authorized. |
| Launch tracking | Verified concept/variant mapping to ad, ad set, campaign and launch date | Identity and evidence requirements documented | Implement the ledger from concept and variant to approved asset, Meta creative and ad IDs. Record verified launch and delivery separately. |
| Performance learning | Weekly decisions and a ROAS-based eligible-test hit rate | CPA-led iteration decisions documented. Eligibility and a recurring readout remain open. | Agree minimum spend, purchases, elapsed days, attribution and cohort rules. Measure the share of eligible tests under $150 and feed the decisions into the next plan. |

The [workflow](../WORKFLOW.md) maps stages and gates, the [process](../PROCESS.md) holds operating requirements, and [project status](../PROJECT_STATUS.md) records historical completion and unresolved items. The [client configuration](CLIENT_CONFIG.md) carries JumpSpeak’s KPI, performance source and Asana rules. Reusable methods stay in the linked upstream Growthub skills.
